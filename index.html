About the artist

I started painting as a kid on the south coast, not long after my parents split and we moved down there. My dad has always fancied himself a bit of an artist — a poet, really — and painting became something we did alongside each other. I kept at it from about grade 2 to grade 7, then, the way these things go, I stopped.

It was nearly fifteen years before I picked up a brush again — about two years ago now, once I was back from studying for my degree in Cape Town. Coming back to it as an adult changed how I paint. I'm less interested in getting a landscape technically "right" than in getting the feeling of it down: big colour, confident brushwork, and a willingness to leave things a little unresolved.

“I'd rather a painting be a little too loud than perfectly quiet.”
