About the artist

My father is a poet who paints. I learned from watching him, on the south coast, when I was seven. I painted until I was twelve, then stopped, the way you stop doing most things you loved as a child.

After finishing my computer science degree at UCT, I had ‘graduated’ my analytical mind at the expense of my artist’s mind, which fell by the wayside. Two years ago I started again — not to recover what I'd lost, but to see what an adult's hand does with a child's habit. What comes out now is less about getting a scene technically right and more about getting it felt.

“The brush remembered what I'd forgotten.”
