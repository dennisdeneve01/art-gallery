About the artist

Painting started for me at seven, on the South Coast of KwaZulu-Natal: salt air, wide skies and colour everywhere you looked. By twelve, the brushes had been packed away.

Then came the “adulting”: a computer science degree at UCT, and years spent building things out of logic. Not long after, I came back to the canvas and found it had waited. These days I care less about getting a scene right, and more about getting it felt.

“The brush remembered what I'd forgotten.”
