Drawings, etchings and paintings

Lucy Hammerschlag

Lucy’s story starts in Vidigal, a village of stone houses in the Portuguese hills. A boat carried her to Mozambique, and from there her family made their way to the south of Johannesburg. That is where my dad met her.

She grew up in a strict, Portuguese-speaking home, with parents who never learnt to read or write. Art was not the obvious path, but it was hers. She left Wits after a year to study at the Johannesburg School of Art, and the drawings she made there are full of figures: bent, reaching, resting, alive.

For years these drawings were kept close by the people who loved her. My dad always hoped the world would see them one day. Now it can.

Shared with love by her family.
