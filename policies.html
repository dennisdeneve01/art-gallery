Prices & policies

Everything you need to know before ordering a canvas print of one of my paintings: what it costs, how it reaches you, and what happens if something goes wrong.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Pricing

Each print is a reproduction of one of my original paintings, printed on canvas and stretched over a wooden frame, ready to hang. The painting continues around the edges. Prices are in South African rand (ZAR) and are the same for every painting; they depend only on the size.

Size (inches)Size (cm)Price
  • Each painting is offered in the sizes that match its shape, so not every size is available for every painting. The sizes for a painting are shown when you choose Buy a canvas print.
  • Delivery is charged separately. The exact delivery cost for your size and country is shown at checkout, before you pay.
  • Payment is taken in rand by card through Paystack. If your card is in another currency, your bank converts the amount.
  • For orders delivered outside the country where the print is made, your customs office may charge import duties or taxes on delivery. These are not included in the price and are paid by the recipient.

Interested in an original painting? Get in touch and I'll tell you what's available.

Shipping & delivery

How it works

Every canvas is printed to order by a professional print partner and sent straight to you by tracked courier. Prints are currently produced outside South Africa.

Delivery times

  • Printing and stretching usually takes 2 to 5 working days.
  • Delivery by courier then usually takes a further 3 to 9 working days, depending on where you are.
  • Most orders arrive within 7 to 14 working days of payment. Customs checks and busy periods can add to this.

Delivery cost

Delivery is worked out for your canvas size and country and shown at checkout before you pay. Nothing is added afterwards by me; any import duties or taxes charged by customs are separate and are the recipient's responsibility.

Where I deliver

. If your country isn't listed, ask me and I'll check.

Tracking and address

  • Once your order is placed you'll see an order reference starting with DH-. Keep it; quote it if you contact me.
  • The courier uses the phone number and email you give at checkout to arrange delivery.
  • Please check your address carefully. If you spot a mistake, contact me straight away; once the parcel has been sent the address can't be changed, and a parcel returned because of a wrong address may cost a second delivery fee.
  • If your order hasn't arrived 30 days after payment, contact me and I'll trace it, and replace or refund it if it's lost.

Refunds & cancellations

If something is wrong with your print

If your canvas arrives damaged, faulty, or not what you ordered, I'll put it right at no cost to you.

  • Email me within 14 days of delivery with your order reference and a few photos of the problem (and of the packaging, if it was damaged in transit).
  • You choose: a free replacement or a full refund, including the delivery cost.
  • You normally won't need to send the damaged canvas back.

Change of mind

Because each canvas is printed specially for your order, in the size you choose, I can't accept returns or give refunds for a change of mind, or for choosing the wrong size. Please check the size and the preview before paying.

Colours on a canvas print can look slightly different from how they appear on a screen, and a small part of the image wraps around the edges of the frame. These are normal for canvas prints and are not treated as faults.

Cancelling an order

Orders go to the printer shortly after payment. If you need to cancel, email me as soon as possible with your order reference. If printing hasn't started I'll cancel the order and refund you in full. Once printing has started, the order can't be cancelled.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card or payment method you used, through Paystack. They are usually processed within 3 working days and can take up to 10 working days to show on your statement, depending on your bank.

Nothing in this policy takes away any rights you have under the law, including the South African Consumer Protection Act.
